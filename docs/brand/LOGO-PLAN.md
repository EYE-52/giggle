## Direction: one monogram, three moods

The looping **GG** is the family mark. Each product keeps the same two loops and changes one thing:

- **Giggle Meet** — unchanged: terracotta GG on cream.
- **Giggle Games** — the GG loops become a playful face: the two "g" bowls are the eyes or the lower loops form a grin, in the Games amber with a pink accent. Still a single-colour line mark, no extra detail. Must work at 16px.
- **Giggle After Dark** — the same GG in warm gold/champagne line on near-black espresso, with one cheeky cue (a wink: one loop half-closed, or a small flame/heart where the loops cross). Premium, playful, adults-only; no moons, stars, zodiac, crystals or tarot cues.

Rules for all three: flat vector, one or two colours, thick rounded stroke matching the Meet mark, rounded-square app tile, legible at 16px, no gradients needed, no text inside the icon. Each ships with a wordmark lockup ("giggle meet", "giggle games", "giggle after dark") in the same rounded geometric type.

## Process

1. Generate concepts in ChatGPT (prompts below), 3–4 options per product.
2. User picks one direction per product.
3. Rebuild the chosen concepts as clean hand-tuned SVGs (monogram stroke shared across all three), plus favicon set, app icons, OG images.
4. Check at 16/32/180/512px, light and dark, next to each other.

## ChatGPT prompts

**Shared preamble:** Flat vector app-icon logo design, presented as a clean grid of 4 options on a plain background, no mockups, no device frames, no extra text. Thick rounded single-weight strokes, rounded-square tile, must stay legible at 16px.

**Giggle Games:** Our video-call app "Giggle Meet" uses a monogram of two hand-drawn, looping lowercase letter g's side by side (GG), terracotta line on cream. Design 4 icon options for its sister product "Giggle Games", a party-games app, that reuse the same looping GG monogram but turn it playful, for example the loops forming a grinning face, a wink, or a game piece, in warm amber #FFB020 with a hot-pink #DE1E6A accent and dark plum #2B1220 line. Simple, friendly, fun, not childish.

**Giggle After Dark:** Same looping two-g GG monogram as above. Design 4 icon options for "Giggle After Dark", the adults-only late-night edition of the Giggle games and video calls. Warm champagne-gold #D8B26E line on near-black espresso #120D0A tile. Cheeky and premium like a cocktail bar sign: e.g. one loop winking, a tiny flame or heart where the loops cross, a subtle neon-sign glow. Absolutely no moon, stars, zodiac, crystal ball or tarot imagery. Tasteful, not explicit.

**Family check:** Show the three app icons side by side: Giggle Meet (terracotta GG on cream), Giggle Games (playful amber GG), Giggle After Dark (gold GG on espresso), so they clearly read as one family.

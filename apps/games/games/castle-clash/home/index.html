Castle Clash

Build a fort, then flatten theirs.

A duel or two crews, one river valley, three rounds of glorious masonry violence. Raise Emberhold or Frostgate from a shared pile of bricks, then turn the cannons around and knock the other lot flat.

Field manual

  1. I Build the fort 45 seconds

    Your crew shares one pile of 100 bricks (200 max). Spend it on walls and towers inside your own half of the 8×8 field — Emberhold digs in on rows 1–3, Frostgate on rows 6–8. Ring your crystal Core with masonry: each adjacent structure gives it weighted cover — wall 1, tower 2, cannon 1, keep 3 — cutting damage to the Core by 15% per weight, capped at 80%.

  2. II Besiege theirs 45 seconds

    Pick a weapon, tap an enemy structure — every shot hits the tapped cell. Every point of damage becomes authority for your crew. A guarded Core can still be hit directly, but cover shrugs off most of the damage, so clearing its guards first often pays. Fire arrows reload fast but hit light; catapults and trebuchets hit harder but slower. Towers and keeps ward the fort against incoming fire (ward caps at 30%); cannons sharpen your own guns. Destroy an enemy structure and the crew earns +10 authority. Removing your own pieces refunds half their brick cost.

  3. III Shatter the Core

    Each Core holds 100 HP. Crack the enemy crystal for +60 authority and the round ends on the spot. If time runs out first, surviving Cores pay their crew half their remaining HP in authority. After 3 rounds, highest authority takes the crown (plus a 50-point winner's purse).

Authority unlocks the good stuff for crews that earn it: cannons at 120, the trebuchet at 300, and the mighty keep at 600 — a durable 3-weight guard at 45 bricks. Unlocks persist across rounds, so later rounds can field new gear; not every game reaches them all. Tearing down your own work refunds half its bricks; the Core itself can never be moved, sold, or upgraded.

House rules host controls
  • Build 15–120s · siege 15–120s · 1–5 rounds — the host sets all three (defaults 45 / 45 / 3).

Dare Roulette

Spin the wheel, do the dare

  • 3–20 players
  • Everyone plays
  • Customizable

How to play

Spin to choose a player and a dare.

The other players vote Done or Chicken.

Completed dares earn points, with streak bonuses.

3–20 players per room.

Make it yours

The host can adjust these before the game starts.

Room settings

  • Rounds 1–12, default 8
  • Pace Options: Relaxed · Normal · Quick. Default: Normal
  • Seconds to perform 10–180, default 60

Custom content

  • Custom dares Bring your own (at least 5)

Start a room

Pick a name, create a room, and invite your friends. No account needed.

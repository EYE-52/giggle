Color Clash

Read it. Or ignore it.

  • 2–20 players
  • Everyone plays
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Follow the prompt without mixing up word and ink.

  • The prompt alternates between ink and word.
  • Answer buttons show colour names too.
  • The small shape marks the ink. Match it to the shape on an answer button.
  • You get one answer per card. Correct and quick earns the most.
All rules

Read the big prompt: tap the ink colour or the written word.

Everyone sees the same 20 cards.

Each card lasts 2.

5 seconds at Normal pace.

A correct answer earns 5 plus up to 5 for speed.

Round scores scale to 100 for the top total.

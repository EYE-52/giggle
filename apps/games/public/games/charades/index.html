Charades

Act it out, guess fast

  • 3–12 players
  • Everyone plays
  • Video call needed for online play
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Guess the acted word fast, and act so others get yours.

  • Enter Send a guess
  • Turn your camera on in Giggle Meet. The actor acts without talking.
  • Guessers type guesses. Correct scores 100, plus up to 50 for speed.
  • The first correct guesser gets 50 more, the second 25.
  • The actor earns 50 for each player who gets it, up to 200.
All rules

Act out your word in Giggle Meet with your camera on — no talking.

Guessers race to name it: a correct guess earns 100 points, more for speed and for being first.

The actor earns 50 for each player who gets it.

3–12 players per room.

Make it yours

The host can adjust these before the game starts.

Room settings

  • Rounds 1–12, default Auto (one per player)
  • Pace Options: Relaxed · Normal · Quick. Default: Normal
  • Seconds per turn 10–600, default 60

Custom content

  • Custom words Bring your own (at least 10, up to 200, each ≤ 60 chars)

Four in a Row

Drop a counter. Set a trap.

  • 2–24 players
  • 2 play · rest watch
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Line up four counters across, up or diagonally.

  • Click Tap Drop a counter in a column
  • ← → Enter Choose a column and drop
  • Red goes first. Counters fall to the lowest open space.
  • A full board without four in a row is a draw.
  • You can resign or offer a draw.
  • With a move timer, running out of time loses the game.
All rules

Red goes first.

Drop a counter into any open column.

The first four in a straight line — across, up, or diagonally — wins.

A full board without four is a draw.

You can resign or offer a draw.

With a move timer, running out of time loses the game.

Two play; everyone else watches.

2–24 players per room. Only 2 play at once — the rest of the room watches (rooms hold up to 32).

Math Dash

Small sums. Big sprint.

  • 2–20 players
  • Everyone plays
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Solve the most problems before the sprint ends.

  • Use your keyboard or the big number pad.
  • A wrong answer pauses you for 2 seconds.
  • Tied counts earn equal points.
  • Easy: sums to 20. Normal: times tables. Hard: two steps.
All rules

Solve as many problems as you can in 45 seconds.

Everyone gets the same sequence.

A wrong answer locks your pad for 2 seconds.

The top count earns 100 points; everyone else earns a share.

2–20 players per room.

Make it yours

The host can adjust these before the game starts.

Room settings

  • Rounds 1–12, default 3
  • Pace Options: Relaxed · Normal · Quick. Default: Normal
  • Difficulty Options: Easy · Normal · Hard. Default: Normal

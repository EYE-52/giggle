Five in a Row

Five stones. A thousand traps.

  • 2–24 players
  • 2 play · rest watch
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Make exactly five stones in a straight line.

  • ↑ ↓ ← → Enter Tap a point Place a stone
  • Black plays first.
  • Six or more stones in one line does not win.
  • White can swap colours just after the opening move.
All rules

Black places the first stone on a 15×15 board.

Take turns placing a stone on an empty intersection.

Exactly five stones in a straight line wins; six or more does not count.

With the swap rule on, White may swap colours instead of placing the second stone.

The new White plays next.

A full board is a draw.

You can resign or offer a draw.

Running out of move time or leaving loses the match.

Two play; everyone else watches.

2–24 players per room. Only 2 play at once — the rest of the room watches (rooms hold up to 32).

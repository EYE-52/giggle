Kalah

Sow a little. Steal a lot.

  • 2–24 players
  • 2 play · rest watch
  • Customizable

How to play

Two players each have six bowls and a score store.

Every bowl starts with four seeds.

Pick a non-empty bowl on your side to sow its seeds around the board, one at a time.

Include your store and skip the other store.

Finish in your store for another turn.

Finish in an empty bowl on your side to capture that seed and the opposite bowl, if the opposite bowl contains seeds.

When either side is empty, the other player adds their remaining seeds to their own store.

Most seeds wins; 24 each is a draw.

You can resign or offer a draw.

With a move timer, running out of time loses the game.

This is the six-bowl, four-seed Kalah preset of Mancala.

2–24 players per room. Only 2 play at once — the rest of the room watches (rooms hold up to 24).

Pig Dice

One more roll? Famous last words.

  • 2–12 players
  • 12 play · rest watch
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Bank the most points by the end of the final round.

  • Tab Enter Roll or Hold Risk the total or bank it
  • A 1 loses only the points from this turn.
  • Hold before the timer runs out to bank safely.
  • Reaching the target gives every seat one equal final chance.
All rules

Roll to add to your turn total.

A 1 loses that total and ends your turn.

Hold to bank it safely.

Reach the target of 50 or 100 to start the final round: everyone gets the same number of turns.

Highest banked total wins, with equal totals sharing a place.

Your 30-second turn timer auto-holds and Pace changes its length.

The server rolls the die.

Leaving forfeits your seat.

With two players you can resign or offer a draw.

2–12 players per room. Only 12 play at once — the rest of the room watches (rooms hold up to 32).

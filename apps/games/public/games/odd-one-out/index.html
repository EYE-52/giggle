Odd One Out

One secret place. One very lost guest.

  • 3–12 players
  • Everyone plays
  • Best with voice
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Find the Outsider without giving away the place.

  • Ask about the place without naming it. Your answer should fit your role.
  • A tied vote lets the Outsider escape. Correct voters earn 100.
  • The Outsider gets one place guess, including a last chance if caught.
All rules

Everyone but the Outsider sees a place and a role.

Ask a player a question out loud; they ask next.

Anyone can call a vote once each round.

Catch the Outsider with a single top vote.

The Outsider can guess the place early for 150 points, or after being caught for 100.

Correct voters earn 100; an escaping Outsider earns 150.

3–12 players per room.

Make it yours

The host can adjust these before the game starts.

Room settings

  • Rounds 1–12, default 4
  • Pace Options: Relaxed · Normal · Quick. Default: Normal

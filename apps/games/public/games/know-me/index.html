Know Me

How well do you know your people?

  • 2–20 players
  • Everyone plays
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Predict what the hot-seat player will pick.

  • The hot seat secretly picks one of four options.
  • Everyone else predicts at the same time. A match earns 100.
  • The hot seat earns 25 per match, or 50 in a two-player game.
All rules

The hot seat picks a true answer while everyone else predicts that choice.

Correct predictions earn 100.

The hot seat earns 25 per correct prediction, capped at 100, or 50 when there is just one predictor.

A missing hot-seat pick skips the round.

Auto gives two turns per player, up to 12 rounds.

2–20 players per room.

Make it yours

The host can adjust these before the game starts.

Room settings

  • Pace Options: Relaxed · Normal · Quick. Default: Normal

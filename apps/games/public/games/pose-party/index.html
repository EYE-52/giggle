Pose Party

Strike a pose live on camera

  • 2–12 players
  • Everyone plays
  • Best on video
  • Customizable

How to play

One player performs the prompt in Giggle Meet and taps Ready before time runs out.

Everyone else watches and votes Yes or No.

An approved pose is worth 100 points plus up to 50 for being ready early; judges who agree with the room get 25.

Turn on your Meet camera so everyone can see your pose.

Nothing is recorded.

2–12 players per room.

Make it yours

The host can adjust these before the game starts.

Room settings

  • Rounds 1–12, default Auto (one turn each)
  • Pace Options: Relaxed · Normal · Quick. Default: Normal
  • Seconds to perform 10–90, default 30
  • Seconds to vote 8–30, default 15

Custom content

  • Custom prompts Bring your own (at least 10, up to 200, each ≤ 120 chars)

Start a room

Pick a name, create a room, and invite your friends. No account needed.

Backronyms

Tiny letters. Ridiculous big ideas.

  • 3–20 players
  • Everyone plays
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Turn the initials into the phrase everyone picks.

  • Use exactly one word per letter, in the same order.
  • Entries stay secret until voting. You cannot vote for your own.
  • Every tied top entry earns 100; picking a top entry adds 25.
All rules

Write a phrase using the shown letters as word initials, in order.

Use exactly one word per letter and no more than 80 characters.

Vote for an anonymous phrase other than your own.

The top entries earn 100, including ties.

Others earn 25 per vote, up to 100.

Voting for any top entry adds 25.

3–20 players per room.

Make it yours

The host can adjust these before the game starts.

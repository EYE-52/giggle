Fleet Duel

Find their fleet before they find yours

  • 2–24 players
  • 2 play · rest watch
  • Customizable

How to play

Place five ships of lengths 5, 4, 3, 3 and 2 horizontally or vertically on your 10×10 harbor.

Ships may touch, but cannot overlap.

Confirm each ship, then Ready.

Take one shot per turn, even after a hit.

Aim at a square, then Fire.

Sink every enemy ship to win.

You can resign at any time.

With a move timer, running out of time on a shot loses the game.

Two play; everyone else watches masked charts.

2–24 players per room. Only 2 play at once — the rest of the room watches (rooms hold up to 24).

Make it yours

The host can adjust these before the game starts.

Room settings

  • Move timer Options: Off · 30 seconds · 60 seconds. Default: Off

Start a room

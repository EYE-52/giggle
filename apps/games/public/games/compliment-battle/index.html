Compliment Battle

Sweetest words win

  • 3–20 players
  • Everyone plays
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Write the compliment the room votes sweetest.

  • Each round names one target. Everyone else writes a kind note.
  • Notes are anonymous. Vote for your favorite, but not your own.
  • The most-voted writer earns 100 points. Tied winners all score.
  • By default, everyone takes one turn as the target.
All rules

Write a private compliment for the target.

Vote on anonymous notes except your own.

Winning writers earn 100 points.

3–20 players per room.

Make it yours

The host can adjust these before the game starts.

Room settings

  • Rounds 1–12, default Auto (one per player)
  • Pace Options: Relaxed · Normal · Quick. Default: Normal
  • Seconds to write 15–600, default 60

Custom content

  • Custom prompts Bring your own (at least 3)

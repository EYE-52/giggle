Word Grid

Trace a word nobody else spots.

  • 2–20 players
  • Everyone plays
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Find long words that no one else finds.

  • Trace touching letters, including diagonals. Do not reuse a tile.
  • Qu counts as two letters on one tile. You can type words too.
  • Shared words cancel out. Longer unique words score more.
  • The top score gets 100; everyone else gets a share of 100.
All rules

Find dictionary words of three or more letters in the shared four-by-four grid within 90 seconds.

Trace touching tiles in any direction, using each tile once per word; Qu is one tile and two letters.

You may also type a word and the server finds its path.

Three or four letters score 1, five score 2, six score 3, seven score 5, and eight or more score 11.

A word found by more than one player scores zero.

The top word score earns 100 round points, with equal scores tied; other players earn their share of 100.

Finish early when everyone is done.

Results show everyone’s words and the five longest possible words.

2–20 players per room.

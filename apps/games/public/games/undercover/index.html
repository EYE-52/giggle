Undercover

Close words. Careful clues. Who sounds different?

  • 3–12 players
  • Everyone plays
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Find the players whose word differs from yours.

  • Use one new clue word. Neither secret word is allowed.
  • There is one Undercover, or two with seven or more players.
  • Learn your side at the end. Tied votes remove nobody.
All rules

Read your secret word; nobody is told their side.

In seat order, give one single-word clue.

Do not use either secret word or repeat a clue.

Vote someone out; a tied top vote removes nobody.

There are up to three clue and vote cycles each round.

Catch every Undercover for 100 each on the majority side.

Undercovers who survive earn 150.

3–12 players per room.

Make it yours

The host can adjust these before the game starts.

Room settings

  • Rounds 1–12, default 4
  • Pace Options: Relaxed · Normal · Quick. Default: Normal

Truth or Dare

Spin, confess or perform

  • 2–20 players
  • Everyone plays
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Win the room's yes vote on your truths and dares.

  • Random card deals one; Room pool vote lets the others pick from 3–4 cards.
  • Ask your own: others write, then pick. No writing? A random card.
  • Mixed picks a style each round. With 2 people, the other player decides.
  • Yes: 100, +25 per extra success (max +100). Split: 50. No or no votes: 0.
  • Winning judges get 25. Prompt votes give no points. Timers move play on.
All rules

Play with 2–20 people.

The spinner picks a player, who chooses a deck and Truth or Dare, or Spin for me.

Prompt style sets how the prompt is picked: Random card deals one, Room pool vote offers 3 cards (4 with 5 or more players), Ask your own lets the others write, and Mixed picks a style each round.

Only the other players pick the prompt and judge the result.

Prompt votes give no points.

Tied prompt votes are settled randomly; no prompt votes means a random option.

A single written prompt is used directly; no writing means a random card.

A yes majority earns 100 plus a streak bonus of 25 per extra success, up to 100 extra.

An even judging split earns 50; no votes earns zero.

Voters on the winning side earn 25.

With two people, the other player alone decides.

All steps have timers.

2–20 players per room.

Make it yours

The host can adjust these before the game starts.

Room settings

  • Rounds 1–12, default Auto (one per player)
  • Pace Options: Relaxed · Normal · Quick. Default: Normal
  • Prompt style Options: Random card · Room pool vote · Ask your own · Mixed. Default: Random card

Pocket Karts

Kart battles with weapons and nitro

  • 2–32 players
  • 32 play · rest watch
  • 3D arena
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Take a rival from 100 HP to 0 to score a knockout. Most knockouts wins.

  • WASD Arrows Stick Drive
  • Space Fire Fire the full weapon payload
  • N Nitro Hold for a speed boost
  • Shift Tyre Hold to drift; release sparks for turbo
  • Grab a box; fire skips the reveal. Each pickup hurts each rival once.
  • Lock missiles ahead. Thunder needs a narrow cone within 35 metres.
  • Blades stay close for 6s. Supernova guards 3s, then bursts within 9m.
  • Dodge Nuke rings. Mine and Oil trap; Frost slows; Boomerang returns; Ram boosts.
  • Raised: cannons climb; portals cross levels. Release drift sparks for turbo.
All rules

Grab a box.

Your weapon appears on the kart and in the bottom slot.

Press Space or Fire to use the full pickup.

Drive with WASD, arrows or the stick; N boosts and Shift drifts.

Every kart has 100 HP.

Take a rival to 0 HP to score a knockout.

You return with full HP and brief protection.

Machine Gun has 30 bullets at 5 damage each.

Tri-Shot has nine pellets at about 17 damage each.

Missile and Cannon deal 65 and 70 on direct hits.

All blasts lose damage toward the edge of their ring.

Mines explode behind you.

Frost Bomb slows nearby rivals.

Blades and Turbo Ram knock out on real contact.

Supernova protects for 3 seconds then bursts.

Nuke has a large warning ring: drive away before it explodes.

Most knockouts wins.

Pick classic or raised arenas in Maps & settings.

2–32 players per room. Only 32 play at once — the rest of the room watches (rooms hold up to 32).

Make it yours

The host can adjust these before the game starts.

Room settings

  • Round length (seconds) 60–900, default 180
  • Stars to win (Star Rush) 8–30, default 15
  • Knockouts to win (Zap Battle) 1–20, default 10
  • Mode Options: Star Rush · Zap Battle · Garage Battle. Default: Zap Battle
  • Arena Options: Rookie Ring · Bumper Blocks · Garden Circuit · Frosty Pass · Neon Yard · Skyport · Foundry · Alpine Courtyard · Frost Quarry · Neon Harbor · Harbor Heights. Default: Garden Circuit
  • Arena palette Options: Daybreak · Sunset · Starlight. Default: Daybreak
  • Win rule Options: Time limit · Score target. Default: Time limit
  • Light cycle Options: Live sunset · Fixed light. Default: Live sunset

Pocket Karts

Kart battles with weapons and nitro

  • 2–24 players
  • 8 play · rest watch
  • 3D arena
  • Customizable

How to play

Drive with WASD/arrows or the thumb stick; Space or the item button fires, and N/Nitro boosts.

Battles start with 100 health: hits deal damage and a knockout at zero health scores.

Direct rockets and guided missiles knock out; nearby blasts deal 40.

A direct Nuke knocks out, with less damage farther from its explosion.

Pulse has 12 rounds and takes 10 hits; Scatter has 5 shells and takes 4 fully connected fans; Rail has 5 rounds and takes 4 hits.

Sentry fires up to 10 rounds and takes 8 hits.

Grab mystery crates; your weapon and remaining rounds appear beside the speedometer.

Press once to fire the full clip: Pulse fires 12 rounds, Rocket fires three missiles, and Mine lays three traps in sequence.

Bomb and Proximity Bomb are thrown dynamite charges: they arm after landing, explode when a rival approaches, and disappear after 12 seconds if unused.

Invincibility protects you for four seconds and knocks out rivals on contact; Spikes spin knives for five seconds, dealing 30 damage with at least half a second between hits on each rival; EMP slows them.

A knockout returns you after two seconds with full health and brief defensive protection; attacking ends protection.

Most knockouts wins.

Choose elevated arenas in Maps & settings for climbs, bridges and jumps, plus Seeker, Bomb, Proximity Bomb, Spikes, Sentry and a rare Nuke; Shift brakes there.

Elevated arenas play Zap Battle with Garage off.

Classic arenas use Shift to drift and also offer Star Rush and Garage Battle.

Rounds last 1 to 5 minutes; with a score target the first to reach it ends the match early.

Places earn 300, 200 and 100 points, and tied drivers share a place.

A driver who leaves or is away for a minute drops out and the others play on.

Up to 8 drive; extra friends watch.

2–24 players per room. Only 8 play at once — the rest of the room watches (rooms hold up to 24).

Make it yours

The host can adjust these before the game starts.

Room settings

  • Round length (seconds) 60–300, default 180
  • Stars to win (Star Rush) 8–30, default 15
  • Knockouts to win (Zap Battle) 1–20, default 10
  • Mode Options: Star Rush · Zap Battle · Garage Battle. Default: Zap Battle
  • Arena Options: Rookie Ring · Bumper Blocks · Garden Circuit · Frosty Pass · Neon Yard · Alpine Courtyard · Frost Quarry · Neon Harbor · Harbor Heights. Default: Garden Circuit
  • Arena palette Options: Daybreak · Sunset · Starlight. Default: Daybreak
  • Win rule Options: Time limit · Score target. Default: Time limit
  • Light cycle Options: Live sunset · Fixed light. Default: Live sunset

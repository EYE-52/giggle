Pocket Karts

Kart battles with weapons and nitro

  • 2–32 players
  • 32 play · rest watch
  • 3D arena
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Take a rival from 100 HP to 0 to score a knockout. Most knockouts wins.

  • WASD Arrows Stick Drive
  • Space Fire Fire the full weapon payload
  • N Nitro Hold for a speed boost
  • Shift Tyre Hold to drift; release sparks for turbo
  • Fire uses the whole pickup. Steer between shots. Drive to lay all three mines.
  • Triple Rockets fly straight. Homing Missile turns toward a rival ahead.
  • Spinning Bumper and Supernova knock out on contact. Supernova shields for 3s.
  • Avoid nuke rings. A fake crate knocks out the first rival who touches it.
  • Raised: cannons climb; portals cross levels. Release drift sparks for turbo.
All rules

Drive with WASD, arrows or the stick.

N boosts and Shift drifts.

Grab a box, then press Space or Fire to use it.

Two direct rocket hits or six Burst Blaster hits knock out a full-health rival.

Keep driving to lay all three trail mines.

Most knockouts wins.

2–32 players per room. Only 32 play at once — the rest of the room watches (rooms hold up to 32).

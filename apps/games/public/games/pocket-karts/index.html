How to play

Goal Take all 3 hearts to score a knockout. Most knockouts wins.

  • WASD Arrows Stick Drive
  • Space Item Fire the full weapon payload
  • N Nitro Hold for a speed boost
  • Shift Tyre Hold to drift; release sparks for turbo
  • Grab a box; fire skips the reveal. Each pickup hurts each rival once.
  • Lock missiles ahead. Thunder needs a narrow cone within 35 metres.
  • Blades stay close for 6s. Supernova guards 3s, then bursts within 9m.
  • Dodge Nuke rings. Mine and Oil trap; Frost slows; Boomerang returns; Ram boosts.
  • Raised: cannons climb; portals cross levels. Release drift sparks for turbo.
All rules

Grab a box for a quick item reveal.

Press Space or the item button to skip it and fire the full payload.

Drive with WASD, arrows or the stick; N boosts.

Each kart has 3 hearts.

There are 13 weapons.

Machine Gun fires straight for 2 seconds; Tri-Shot fires three tight fans.

Missiles lock only ahead at launch, then home gently.

Thunder needs a 30 degree forward cone within 35 metres; otherwise it misses.

Cannon hits take 2 hearts, nearby splash takes 1.

Blades orbit locally for 6 seconds; the returning Boomerang is harmless.

Supernova blocks every hit for 3 seconds, then bursts for 2 hearts within 9 metres.

A Nuke knocks out rivals in its warning ring.

Mine, Oil Slick, Frost Bomb, Thunder Strike, Boomerang and Turbo Ram each take 1 heart.

Each pickup hurts each rival once; each mine or oil trap is separate.

Most knockouts wins; you return after 2 seconds with full hearts and brief protection.

Choose classic or raised arenas in Maps & settings.

Classic arenas, Skyport and Foundry allow 2 to 32 drivers; Alpine Courtyard, Frost Quarry, Neon Harbor and Harbor Heights allow 2 to 8.

Extra friends watch.

Skyport is a three-floor rooftop car park with a spiral ramp, sky bridges, a helipad and a roof jump.

Foundry has three floors with furnace tunnels, moving conveyor lanes, a crane yard and a high gantry loop; avoid the molten slag pit.

The other raised arenas have connected decks, ramps and underpasses.

Raised arenas use Zap Battle with Garage off; classic arenas also offer Star Rush and Garage Battle.

Hold Shift or the tyre button in a turn to drift on every arena.

Blue, orange and purple sparks give stronger turbo boosts when you release.

Ice and gravel have different grip.

Raised arenas have bright launch cannons to upper decks and paired portals between levels; drive into the cannon rear or portal ring.

Brief cooldowns prevent repeat loops.

Common: Machine Gun, Tri-Shot, Mine, Oil Slick, Boomerang.

Uncommon: Homing Missile, Cannon, Spinning Blades, Frost Bomb, Thunder Strike, Turbo Ram.

Rare: Supernova, Nuke.

2–32 players per room. Only 32 play at once — the rest of the room watches (rooms hold up to 32).

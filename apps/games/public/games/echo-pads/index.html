Echo Pads

Watch the beat. Tap it back.

  • 2–20 players
  • Everyone plays
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Remember the longest sequence.

  • Every pad has a shape and a sound as well as a colour.
  • Wait until the prompt says to repeat.
  • You have 3 seconds plus 0.8 seconds per step, scaled by Pace.
All rules

Watch the four pads light up, then repeat the sequence.

It starts at 3 steps and grows by one each level.

A miss knocks you out of the round.

Last survivors who clear a level earn 100; others earn a share for the levels cleared.

Clear level 15 to finish.

2–20 players per room.

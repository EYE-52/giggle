Mega Tic-Tac-Toe

Win the small. Think big.

  • 2–24 players
  • 2 play · rest watch
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Win three small boards in a row.

  • ↑ ↓ ← → Enter Tap a cell Place your X or O
  • The bright rim marks the boards you can play on.
  • Your cell sends the next player to that small board.
  • A closed destination lets the next player go anywhere.
All rules

Play X or O on nine small boards.

The cell you choose sends your opponent to that small board.

If that board is won or full, they may play on any open board.

Win three cells in a row to claim a small board, then claim three small boards in a row to win.

No moves left means a draw.

You can resign or offer a draw.

Running out of move time or leaving loses the match.

Two play; everyone else watches.

2–24 players per room. Only 2 play at once — the rest of the room watches (rooms hold up to 32).

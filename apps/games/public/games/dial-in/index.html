Dial In

Give a clue, find the spot

  • 2–20 players
  • Everyone plays
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Guide the room to a secret spot on a spectrum.

  • Take turns giving one short clue for a secret target.
  • Move the dial, then lock your guess. Closer earns more.
  • The Dialer earns the average of the guessing points.
All rules

The Dialer sees a secret target from 0 to 100 and gives a clue on a spectrum.

Everyone else locks a guess.

Within 4 scores 100, within 10 scores 50, and within 18 scores 25.

The Dialer earns the rounded average.

A missing clue skips the round.

2–20 players per room.

Make it yours

The host can adjust these before the game starts.

Room settings

  • Rounds 1–20, default One turn each
  • Pace Options: Relaxed · Normal · Quick. Default: Normal

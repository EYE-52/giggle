Find your shared brainwave

  • 2–20 players

How to play

Goal Find the answer other people are thinking of.

  • Write one short answer. Keep it simple.
  • The biggest matching piles earn 100; other matches earn 50.
  • Solo answers score zero. Tied biggest piles share first place.
All rules

Everyone writes a short answer to the same prompt.

Matching answers become sticker piles.

The biggest piles with at least two people score 100 each.

Other matches score 50 each.

Solo answers score zero.

Simple plurals and leading a, an or the are ignored.

2–20 players per room.

Make it yours

The host can adjust these before the game starts.

Room settings

  • Rounds 1–12, default 8
  • Pace Options: Relaxed · Normal · Quick. Default: Normal

Custom content

  • Custom prompts Bring your own (at least 5, up to 200, each ≤ 140 chars)

  • 2–24 players

  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Checkmate your opponent's king.

  • Click Tap Pick a piece, then a square
  • ↑ ↓ ← → Enter Move the cursor and select
  • Esc Cancel your selection
  • The first two players take white and black. Everyone else watches.
  • Tap a piece to see its legal moves.
  • A pawn reaching the last rank lets you choose its promotion.
  • You can resign or offer a draw.
  • With a time control or move timer, running out of time loses.
All rules

The first two players take white and black; everyone else watches.

Tap a piece to see its legal moves, then tap a destination.

Pawns promote with a chooser.

You can resign or offer a draw.

With a time control or a move timer, running out of time loses the game.

2–24 players per room. Only 2 play at once — the rest of the room watches (rooms hold up to 32).

Make it yours

The host can adjust these before the game starts.

Room settings

  • Time control Options: Off · 3 minutes · 5 minutes · 10 minutes. Default: Off
  • Move timer Options: Off · 30 seconds · 60 seconds. Default: Off
  • Board appearance Options: Mono · Blue · Green. Default: Mono

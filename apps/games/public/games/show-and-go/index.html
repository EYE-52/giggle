Show & Go

Grab it, show it, win the votes

  • 2–12 players
  • Everyone plays
  • Video call needed for online play
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Show a convincing find and win the room's approval.

  • Everyone hunts for the same everyday object at once.
  • Show it on your Meet camera, then tap I found it.
  • Vote Yes or No on each find. You never vote on your own.
  • An approved find earns 100, plus up to 50 for being ready early.
  • Judges who agree with the room earn 25 each, up to 50 a round.
All rules

Everyone hunts the same everyday object and shows it in Giggle Meet, then taps Ready.

Peers vote Yes on convincing finds; you never vote for yourself.

An approved find is worth 100 points plus up to 50 for being ready early; judges who agree with the room get 25.

Turn on your Meet camera so everyone can see your find.

Nothing is recorded.

2–12 players per room.

Make it yours

The host can adjust these before the game starts.

Room settings

  • Rounds 1–12, default 4
  • Pace Options: Relaxed · Normal · Quick. Default: Normal
  • Seconds to perform 10–300, default 30
  • Seconds to vote 8–180, default 15

Custom content

  • Custom prompts Bring your own (at least 10, up to 200, each ≤ 120 chars)

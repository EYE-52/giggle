How to play

Goal Be the first to find the hidden word.

  • Each guess shows how close it is in meaning, with a rank.
  • Lower ranks are closer. Rank 1 is the exact word.
  • Only the exact word wins. The first solver earns 100 to 200 points.
  • Hints reveal a close word but cost 50 points each, up to twice.
  • Similarity is about meaning, not spelling.
All rules

Everyone races to find the same hidden everyday English word.

Each guess gets real AI word-embedding cosine similarity and a vocabulary rank: lower ranks are closer, and rank 1 is the exact answer.

Share ideas on the call!

Only the exact word wins.

The first solver earns 100–200 points depending on time remaining.

Optional hints reveal a close word and reduce the round prize by 50 points each, at most twice.

Three 90-second rounds by default.

Similarity measures meaning, not spelling; the model can make surprising associations.

Guesses must be in our 1,507-word vocabulary.

Custom targets use supported vocabulary words only; unsupported targets are skipped with a notice.

2–20 players per room.

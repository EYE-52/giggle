Reversi

Turn the table, one disc at a time

  • 2–24 players
  • 2 play · rest watch
  • Customizable

How to play

Charcoal moves first.

Place a disc on a legal dot to trap ivory discs between yours, in any straight line.

All trapped discs flip.

If you have no move, your turn passes automatically.

When neither player can move, the most discs wins.

You can resign or offer a draw.

With a move timer, running out of time loses the game.

Two play; everyone else watches.

2–24 players per room. Only 2 play at once — the rest of the room watches (rooms hold up to 24).

Make it yours

The host can adjust these before the game starts.

Room settings

  • Move timer Options: Off · 30 seconds · 60 seconds. Default: Off

Bluff

Fake answers, find the truth

  • 3–20 players
  • Everyone plays
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Find the real answer, and fool others with your fake.

  • Enter Send your fake answer
  • A fact is shown with a blank. Write a believable fake to fill it.
  • Then pick the real answer from all the options (not your own fake).
  • Picking the truth scores 100.
  • Each player who picks your fake gives you 50, up to 100.
  • A fake that matches the real answer is not accepted.
All rules

Write a fake answer, then pick the truth from the mix.

Finding the truth is worth 100; each player your bluff fools is worth 50 (up to 100).

3–20 players per room.

Make it yours

The host can adjust these before the game starts.

Room settings

  • Rounds 1–12, default 6
  • Pace Options: Relaxed · Normal · Quick. Default: Normal
  • Seconds to write 15–600, default 45

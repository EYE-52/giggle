Sketchy Impostor

One pen has no idea what it is drawing.

  • 4–10 players
  • Everyone plays
  • Live drawing
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Draw just enough to prove you know the word.

  • You have one continuous stroke per turn. Lifting your finger ends the turn.
  • Two laps share one canvas. Each player has a different ink colour.
  • A tied vote catches nobody. Votes stay hidden until voting ends.
  • If caught, the Impostor has one guess and 20 seconds to steal the round.
All rules

Everyone but the Impostor sees a secret word.

The Impostor sees only its category.

Take two laps in seat order: add one continuous stroke on your turn.

Vote for the Impostor.

A unique top vote catches them, but they can steal 150 points by guessing the word.

If that guess is wrong, correct voters get 100 each.

An uncaught Impostor gets 150.

4–10 players per room.

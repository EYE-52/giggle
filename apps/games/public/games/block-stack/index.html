Block Stack

One tap. A smaller landing. A taller tower.

  • 2–12 players
  • Everyone plays
  • 3D arena
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Keep a wide landing and build the tallest tower.

  • Space Enter Drop button Drop your block
  • Your tower has a bright base. Everyone drops independently.
  • Wait for 3–2–1. The first swing starts directly over your base.
  • Drop with the button, Space or Enter. Follow the landing guide.
  • Tied tallest towers each earn 100. A missed tower keeps its height.
All rules

Wait for 3–2–1, then tap Drop as the sliding block lines up with your tower.

The first block starts over your base.

Overhang is trimmed off and the smaller block carries on.

A perfect drop within three percent keeps its full size.

A full miss ends your run.

Everyone builds at once for 60 seconds after the countdown.

The tallest scores 100; other towers score their height as a share of the best.

Play three rounds by default.

2–12 players per room.

Checkers

Set a trap. Make a king.

  • 2–24 players
  • 2 play · rest watch
  • Customizable

How to play

Two players take red and charcoal; friends can watch.

English rules: men move and jump diagonally forward; kings move and jump in both directions without flying.

Captures are compulsory, and further jumps must use the same piece.

Choose any available capture path.

Reaching the far edge makes a king and ends the turn.

Win when your opponent has no pieces or no legal moves.

Repeated positions three times or 40 turns each without a capture or a man moving draw the game.

You can also resign or agree to a draw.

With a move timer, running out of time loses the game.

2–24 players per room. Only 2 play at once — the rest of the room watches (rooms hold up to 24).

Word Countdown

Longest word from 9 letters

  • 2–20 players
  • Everyone plays
  • Customizable

How to play

Build a supported English word with 3–9 letters from the nine tiles before time runs out.

Every letter can be used only as many times as it appears.

Save to keep editing, lock in to submit, or pass.

Unsupported words get feedback while the clock keeps running.

A valid word earns 50 points plus 10 per letter (five letters: 100); everyone tied for the longest earns 50 more.

Each tray has a valid word.

The host may add extra accepted words to the offline dictionary.

2–20 players per room.

Make it yours

The host can adjust these before the game starts.

Room settings

  • Rounds 1–12, default 4
  • Pace Options: Relaxed · Normal · Quick. Default: Normal
  • Seconds to build 15–90, default 30

Custom content

  • Extra accepted words (3–9 letters) Bring your own (at least 1, up to 200, each ≤ 9 chars)

Start a room

Pick a name, create a room, and invite your friends. No account needed.

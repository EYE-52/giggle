Word Chain

Stay linked, never repeat

  • 3–20 players
  • Everyone plays
  • Customizable

How to play

On your turn, say a supported English word related to the last word before time runs out.

Unsupported spellings get feedback and you may retry on the same clock.

A repeat, a blank or running out of time costs a life; with no lives left you are out.

The offline dictionary checks spelling; the room judges the relationship.

A majority of the players still in may challenge the previous word, which costs its author a life.

Pace scales the turn time.

Last player standing earns 300 points; the next places earn 200 and 100.

3–20 players per room.

Make it yours

The host can adjust these before the game starts.

Room settings

  • Pace Options: Relaxed · Normal · Quick. Default: Normal
  • Lives 1–3, default 1
  • Seconds per turn 5–60, default 10

Custom content

  • Custom starter words Bring your own (at least 3, each ≤ 24 chars)
  • Extra accepted words (letters A–Z) Bring your own (at least 1, up to 200, each ≤ 24 chars)

Start a room

Pick a name, create a room, and invite your friends. No account needed.

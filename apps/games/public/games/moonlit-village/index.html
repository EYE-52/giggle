Moonlit Village

Keep the lanterns glowing. Find the wolves.

  • 5–16 players
  • Everyone plays
  • Best with voice
  • Customizable

How to play

Goal Find the wolves, or quietly take over the village.

  • Night choices are secret. Wolves can see their pack.
  • Tap Ready to shorten discussion once everyone is ready.
  • A tied day vote sends nobody home. Out players can watch all roles.
All rules

At night wolves choose whose lantern to take, the Seer checks one side and the Healer protects one player.

The Healer cannot protect the same player on consecutive nights.

Discuss by day, then vote someone home; tied votes send nobody home.

Out players watch with all roles visible.

The village wins with no wolves left; wolves win when they match or outnumber the others.

The winning side earns 300 each; surviving losers earn 100.

5–16 players per room.

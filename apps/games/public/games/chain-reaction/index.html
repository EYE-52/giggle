Chain Reaction

One tiny orb. A very big BOOM.

  • 2–24 players
  • 6 play · rest watch
  • Customizable

How to play

Two to six players take turns adding an orb to an empty cell or a cell they own.

Corners burst at 2, edges at 3, and inner cells at 4.

Each burst sends an orb to every neighboring cell and captures it, setting off chain reactions.

Everyone gets one turn before players with no orbs are eliminated.

Be the last owner standing!

With a move timer, a missed turn is skipped.

A player who leaves drops out; extra friends watch.

2–24 players per room. Only 6 play at once — the rest of the room watches (rooms hold up to 24).

Make it yours

The host can adjust these before the game starts.

Room settings

  • Board Options: Compact · Standard. Default: Compact
  • Move timer Options: Off · 30 seconds · 60 seconds. Default: Off
  • Orb palette Options: Candy · Ink. Default: Candy

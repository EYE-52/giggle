How practice works

Player 1 makes an ordinary online room and the other seats join it. Open the game in Player 1, then switch seats to act, answer and vote. The game’s normal rules and timers still apply.

This is not an offline or private mode. Anyone you give the room code can join. Seeing each seat’s secrets means the scores are for your walkthrough, not a fair contest. Camera and microphone access are blocked in these practice frames.

End practice to leave every seat. Your normal rooms and saved player name stay separate.

Current 96px portraits are rendered from the twelve Gear models by scripts/build-kart-pickup-art.py. All eleven unique pickup silhouettes include original Meshy artwork; the fake crate is derived from the common loot crate. Source task IDs, hashes and triangle counts are recorded in manifest.json and the source task file. See ../ASSETS.md for source models, permissions and rebuild instructions.

Machine Gun, Tri-Shot, Homing Missile, Cannon, Spinning Blades and Supernova: original Giggle SVG icons at 96px, authored directly as vector paths in this folder. No external images. The other current weapon icons retain their existing model renders and credits.
